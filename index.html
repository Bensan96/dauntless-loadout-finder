Data: Dauntless Builder Reforged (game data 1.14.6, the newest available). Armor has one perk and one typed cell slot, weapons have two slots, the lantern has an Insight slot, and a perk caps at +6. A cell only fits a slot of its own family; Prismatic slots take any cell. Legendary weapons have two Prismatic slots and copy the perk of the weapon they are bonded with. Omnicells and weapon parts don’t change perk totals and aren’t included.
